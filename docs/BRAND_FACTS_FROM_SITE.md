## The origin story (this is the crown jewel)

**The Secret Peanuts.** One winter evening, **Grandpa Carney** was perfecting a peanut
brittle recipe — secret ingredients, freshly churned butter — in his cottage kitchen. While
he stepped out for firewood, a **chipmunk** got in, climbed to the shelf where the Virginia
red-skinned peanuts were kept, and spilled them **straight into the mixing bowl**. It fled
with full cheeks, one peanut escaping. Grandpa came back to find his recipe transformed —
accidental **butter toffee peanuts**. He made them every year after, and left a batch
outside his door as thanks to his uninvited helper.

**Why this matters enormously:** the chipmunk isn't decoration — **the chipmunk invented the
signature product.** Chippy the mascot, the critters on the island, the "Secret" in Secret
Peanuts, and the best-selling product all come from one story. Any journey we build should
treat the chipmunk as the *narrator//guide*, not ambient wildlife.

## Brand voice — "The 5-Step Program" (verbatim)

A parody of a 12-step recovery program. Perfect calibration for how playful the brand is
allowed to be:

1. "We admit that we are simply powerless over our desire for Secret Peanuts."
2. "It seems like every time we go somewhere, we come across Simply Nuts & More and covet Secret Peanuts."
3. "We found that trying to ignore this desire was simply contrary to reason and frankly eating them was very good for us."
4. "We finally made a list of all those friends and family we've kept this a secret from."
5. "After making amends and experiencing this awakening, we now share the story of Secret Peanuts with everyone we meet. It is simply the right thing to do."
