# Simply Nuts & More Brand + UX Strategy (v1)

## North Star
Build a warm, premium digital experience that turns manual gifting coordination into a repeatable, low-friction ordering system.

## Positioning
Simply Nuts & More is the family-run gifting partner that makes custom appreciation gifts effortless, from engraving to direct-to-recipient shipping.

## Brand Pillars
- Heritage with Heart: Family business credibility since 1987.
- Premium Without Pretension: High quality products at approachable pricing.
- Custom Made Easy: Guided workflows instead of email ping-pong.
- Human Support, Always: Design cleanup and white-glove help built into the flow.

## Primary Users
- Corporate buyers: need speed, approvals, and reliable bulk shipping.
- Retail shoppers: want quality, value, and easy seasonal shopping.

## UX Principles
- Clarity first: one primary action per screen.
- Trust visible: quality and fulfillment details always in view.
- Delight in moments: motion where it improves confidence.
- No dead ends: autosave, resume links, and human handoff options.

## IA Overview
- Shop by Pound
- Corporate Gifts
- Customize
- Bulk Orders
- Events
- About

## Motion Strategy
- Lightweight motion globally.
- 3D/WebGL focused on the customizer only.
- Respect reduced-motion system settings.

## KPI Starter Set
- Corporate draft completion rate
- Abandonment recovery rate
- Average order value by segment
- Repeat corporate account rate
- Time-to-submit for first corporate order
